## Autonomous Rescue Extraction Drone 3D Design
Autonomous Rescue &amp; Extraction Drone is an AI-powered octocopter drone designed to locate victims, deploy a smart human harness, and guide them to safety, at the same time providing immediate medical diagnosis in flood and landslide disaster zones.

![A.R.E.D. Hero Image](https://github.com/ngai-jeremy/Autonomous-Rescue-Extraction-Drone/blob/main/images/Hero%20Image.jpg)

## Key Features
* **Foldable Carbon-Fiber Hull:** Heavy-duty articulated mechanical joints allow the arms to be folded for easy storage and transport.
* **Coaxial 8-Rotor System:** Coaxial dual-motor layout provides heavy lifting for human harness.
* **Smart Victim Harness:** Full-body harness to secure the victim with a 6.3-inch medical diagnosis screen, hydration pack, and biometric mesh sensors (SpO2 & HR).
* **AI Sensor Fusion & Load Balancing:** Fuses LiDAR, UWB Radar, and Thermal Camera to locate victim and continuously adjusting motor thrust to prevent rope pendulum effect.
